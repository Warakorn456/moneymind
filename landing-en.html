Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers, no fine print — full detail always lives in our real policies

Is this app really free? Will I get charged later?
Right now, all core features are free (logging income/expenses, receipt scanning, tax calculation, portfolio tracking) — no card or payment required to get started.
How safe is my financial data?
Passwords are irreversibly hashed, your data is never sold or shared, and only you can access it — see the full detail in the Data Security section above.
Does this app connect directly to my bank account?
No — and we never ask for your banking password. The app only imports transactions from the bank transaction alert emails your bank sends you (this is not an Open Banking connection). You can also just log transactions yourself.
Can I delete my data if I stop using the app?
Anytime. The financial data you enter is always yours — export or delete all of it from the in-app settings page, no need to ask anyone — see Terms of Service.
I have an issue or a suggestion — how do I reach you?
Email us directly at warakornbest6@gmail.com — we read and respond to every message, and use it to keep improving the app.
Is this app a financial advisor?
No. MoneyMind is not a financial, investment, tax, or legal advisor. The numbers and suggestions in the app (including from the AI) are estimates for personal planning purposes only. Always check with a licensed professional before making real financial decisions.
